# NAMNIA Product Vision

## Problem

Many SMEs depend heavily on external platforms such as social media and marketplaces to acquire and communicate with customers.

They often lack their own systems for:

- customer data
- customer loyalty
- customer retention
- marketing automation
- analytics
- personalized marketing

## NAMNIA's Goal

NAMNIA aims to provide SMEs with a simple but smart customer growth platform.

## Core Idea

Help businesses:

1. Acquire customers
2. Understand customers
3. Retain customers
4. Automate marketing
5. Analyze customer behavior
6. Use AI to make better marketing decisions

## Initial Modules

### 1. CRM
Customer profiles and customer data.

### 2. Loyalty
Points, membership levels, rewards and coupons.

### 3. Booking
Appointments and service bookings.

### 4. Marketing Automation
Automated campaigns and customer journeys.

### 5. Analytics
Customer, campaign and retention dashboards.

### 6. AI Intelligence
Recommendations, segmentation and predictive models.
